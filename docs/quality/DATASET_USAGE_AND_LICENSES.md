# Dataset Usage and Licences

No dataset was downloaded during this audit. Nothing below is legal advice; each entry states
what the publisher's own material says and what must be confirmed before use.

## AI City Challenge 2021 Track 4

- **Published as:** challenge research data, linked from the official download page (Google Drive).
- **Obligation:** accept the challenge terms; cite the AI City Challenge papers and the dataset
  authors in any publication or demo.
- **Caution:** research access does not automatically grant commercial redistribution rights.
  Confirm with the organisers before shipping footage in a commercial product.
- **In this repo:** does not ship. Only a registry entry and download instructions.

## RDD2022 / CRDDC2022 (India)

- **Published by:** the RoadDamageDetector authors (Sekilab et al.).
- **Obligation:** attribution to the RDD2022/CRDDC2022 authors and the accompanying paper;
  the repository's stated terms govern redistribution.
- **Caution:** benchmark datasets frequently restrict commercial use; verify before any
  non-research deployment.
- **In this repo:** does not ship. Parser and class mapping only.

## BDD100K

- **Published by:** Berkeley DeepDrive.
- **Obligation:** account registration and explicit acceptance of the BDD100K licence at
  download time.
- **Caution:** the licence has conditions that educational access alone does not satisfy; do not
  assume commercial rights.
- **In this repo:** does not ship. Registry entry and instructions only.

## Model weights

- The YOLO adapter is written against the Ultralytics API. **Ultralytics YOLOv8 weights and code
  are AGPL-3.0** by default — a real constraint for a closed-source municipal deployment.
  Resolve licensing (commercial licence or a permissively licensed model) before shipping.
- No weights are committed. `*.pt`, `*.pth` and `*.onnx` are git-ignored.
- Record model name, version and weights SHA-256 in `ai_jobs.weights_sha256` when a real model
  is installed; the column already exists.

## Synthetic assets in this repo

- `scripts/generate_demo_clip.py` produces the synthetic clip; it is generated, not licensed
  third-party footage, and every page labels it as synthetic.
- The fixture dataset is generated by `scripts/datasets/build_fixtures.py` and is free of
  third-party content.
